## Limitations

- Repository control or creation does not conclusively prove authorship or legal ownership.
- A hash proves file identity relative to a known digest, not who created the file.
- A transparency-log entry can support evidence that a digest/signing event existed by a time, but does not grant copyright, trademark, patent rights, or worldwide exclusivity.
- Copyright generally protects qualifying expression such as source code, not an abstract idea or product name as such.
- Trademark and patent rights depend on jurisdiction, eligibility, prior rights, procedure, and sometimes fees. No registration or filing is claimed here.
